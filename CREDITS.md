## Voices

The folders called `voice/en/`, in `core/` and each game with its own lines, were rendered on this computer with [Kokoro 82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) (Apache 2.0). See `tools/render_voice.py`.

The folders called `voice/ka/` were rendered with Microsoft's `ka-GE-EkaNeural` and `ka-GE-GiorgiNeural` voices, through the open-source [edge-tts](https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts) tool. See `tools/render_voice_ka.py`. **Check the terms before publishing the games.** edge-tts uses the online service behind Microsoft Edge's Read Aloud feature, which isn't an official interface, and whether its output can be shipped in a public product hasn't been confirmed. The same two voices are sold through [Azure AI Speech](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/ai-services/speech-service/language-support), which is the official route, and the lines would only need rendering again.

## Characters

All ten characters in `core/body/roster.js`, and the worlds of Leap Trail, Sky Wings and the games with a mission are original designs made for these games.
